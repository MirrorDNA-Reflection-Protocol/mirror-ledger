### Abstract

India's AI build-out will depend on more than models and GPUs. This thesis
argues that the binding constraint is firm, high-quality electricity delivered
to the correct physical node. It maps the resulting sequence—from substations,
transmission, transformers, cables and meters through firm generation,
storage and grid software—and publishes six dated forecasts so the argument
can be scored against evidence through 2032.
