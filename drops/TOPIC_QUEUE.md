# Research Drop Topic Queue

**As of:** 2026-08-11  
**Status:** Candidate questions and source seeds, not published conclusions.

Every scheduled run must reopen each source, check its publication date and

## Wednesday — international AI

### Candidate question

Is the international AI race shifting from access to chips toward the ability
to secure power, grid connections, measurement and credible project execution?

### Working hypothesis — Inference

Sovereign compute plans increasingly depend on physical power-system capacity
and queue discipline. The brief should test this across at least three regions
and distinguish announced capacity from connected, financed capacity.

### Primary-source seeds

- [IEA — Key Questions on Energy and AI (2026)](https://www.iea.org/reports/key-questions-on-energy-and-ai)
- [European Commission — European Tech Sovereignty (2026)](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/communication-european-tech-sovereignty-accompanied-eu-open-source-strategy)
- [European Commission/EIB — AI Gigafactories memorandum](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/memorandum-understanding-ai-gigafactories)
- [US DOE — 2026 Draft National Transmission Needs Study](https://www.energy.gov/oe/articles/does-office-electricity-publishes-2026-draft-national-transmission-needs-study)
- [US DOE — Monitoring Oscillations from Large Data Centers](https://www.energy.gov/oe/articles/monitoring-oscillations-large-data-centers)
- [Ofgem — Proposed data-centre connection reforms](https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/consultation/proposed-data-centre-connection-reforms)

### Disconfirming evidence to seek

- proof that compute or chips, rather than connection-ready power, remain the
  binding constraint across the sampled regions;
- material reductions in connection delays without corresponding network work;
- operating gigafactory capacity that materially exceeds financed announcements.

## Friday — crypto

### Candidate question

Are dollar stablecoins becoming a parallel foreign-exchange and settlement
layer in emerging markets, and where do public transfer metrics overstate real
economic use?

### Working hypothesis — Estimate

Stablecoins are broadening beyond crypto-market settlement, but raw transfer
counts can materially misclassify activity and should not be equated with
payments or adoption. The brief must keep utility, dollarisation risk and
measurement error visible together.

### Primary-source seeds

- [BIS Papers 170 — Stablecoins and the international monetary system](https://www.bis.org/publ/bppdf/bispap170.htm)
- [BIS Working Paper 1340 — Stablecoin flows and FX spillovers](https://www.bis.org/publ/work1340.htm)
- [BIS Working Paper 1359 — Anatomy of stablecoin transactions](https://www.bis.org/publ/work1359.htm)
- [IMF — Tokenized Finance and Money](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2026/05/11/sp051126-tokenized-finance-and-money)
- [IMF — April 2026 Global Financial Stability Report](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/gfsr/2026/april/english/text.pdf)
- [BIS/FSI — Implementation of the global cryptoasset framework](https://www.bis.org/fsi/fsisummaries/exsum_24901.htm)

### Disconfirming evidence to seek

- high-quality payment-level evidence showing broad real-economy use rather
  than exchange, bridge or smart-contract event activity;
- declining non-dollar-to-stablecoin conversion activity;
- regulation that measurably reduces cross-border or parallel-FX use.

## Sunday — Active Mirror

### Candidate question

What evidence distinguishes emergent reflection, metacognitive behavior and a
persistent self-model from a defensible claim of machine consciousness?

### Working hypothesis — Inference

Reflective language and stable self-description are observable behaviors, not
proof of subjective experience. A credible assessment needs triangulation across
behavioral controls, mechanisms, perturbations and observer-confound tests.

### Research-source seeds

- [Butlin et al. — Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708)
- [AAAI 2026 — Triangulating Evidence for Machine Consciousness Claims](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI-SS/article/view/42552)
- [Computers in Human Behavior Reports — Features shaping perceived AI consciousness](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2451958825003161)
- [Phua — Testing consciousness theories with ablations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19155)
- [Metacognitive AI position paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15567)

### Disconfirming evidence to seek

- replicated, causally identified indicators that cannot be explained by
  role-play, prompt framing, training imitation or observer attribution;
- mechanistic evidence linking a stable self-model to theory-specific
  consciousness indicators rather than task performance alone;
- robustness under architecture ablation, adversarial prompting and
  cross-model replication.

## Citation-production rule

Weekly drops should earn attention through falsifiable claims and transparent
receipts. A monthly synthesis may be promoted to a versioned PDF and DOI only
after exact content review. Citation is an outcome of reusable evidence, stable
metadata and independent uptake—not a metric the cadence can guarantee.
